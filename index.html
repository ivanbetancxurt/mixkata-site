What you train

Four things every mix depends on.

  1. 01

    EQ

    Hear where a sound was boosted or cut: low, mid, or high.

  2. 02

    Compression

    Notice when dynamics get squashed, and when they don’t.

  3. 03

    Loudness

    Judge level differences without being fooled by louder-is-better.

  4. 04

    Stereo width

    Hear a mix open up or narrow in the stereo field.
