The short version: If you join the waitlist, we collect your email address only. We use it to send MixKata launch updates. You can unsubscribe anytime.

When you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter. We do not ask for your name, and we do not collect any other personal information through this form.

We use your email only to send updates about MixKata, including when the app is available. We do not sell or rent your email, and we do not use it for advertising.

The waitlist form is sent through FormSubmit, a form delivery service, which forwards your email to us. FormSubmit's handling of submissions is described in its own privacy policy.

Your choices

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
